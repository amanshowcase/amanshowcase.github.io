MY STORY

Connecting Experience with Perspective

My professional journey has evolved across recruitment, technical support, enterprise service delivery and business operations. Each stage has contributed to my understanding of how people, processes and technology work together in an organisation.

I began my career in recruitment operations, gaining exposure to candidate sourcing, preliminary screening, interview coordination, recruitment documentation and client communication.

My subsequent experience in enterprise support and service delivery strengthened my approach to problem-solving, process compliance, stakeholder communication and operational coordination.

My more recent exposure to business operations and digital transformation, alongside my MBA studies in Human Resource Management, has broadened my perspective on management, organisational processes and strategic decision-making.
