RESEARCH PERSPECTIVE

Connecting Technology with the Human Side of Organisations

AI integration is not solely a technology consideration. It also raises questions about employee learning, workplace adaptation and the support organisations provide. This research brings these dimensions together, examining the relevance of HRD interventions and employee development to organisational performance.
